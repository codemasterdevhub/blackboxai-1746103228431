OUR STORY

Celebrating Album Art Since 2015

What began as a personal passion project has grown into the web's most comprehensive archive of exceptional album artwork.

"Music is the art of thinking with sounds." – Jules Combarieu
500+ Albums • 200+ Artists • 15 Genres

Our Mission

We believe album artwork is an essential part of the musical experience. In an era of digital streaming, we aim to preserve and celebrate the visual artistry that accompanies great music.

The Collection

Our carefully curated selection spans decades and genres, from classic vinyl sleeves to contemporary digital releases. Each piece tells a story and enhances the listening experience.

Community

We collaborate with artists, designers, and music lovers worldwide to document and appreciate the often overlooked art of album design.

"Album art is the visual soul of music - a gateway to the auditory experience within."
